## 2. Search by photo + words — DONE in the backend and the search box (not committed)
**Backend design** (`GET /feed/products|services?photo_token=..&q=..`, see `sqrtx/API.md` "Photo + words"): the photo chooses the pool (200 nearest main photos, the photo search's lines with drop 0.30), four DBSF lists order it: photo to photo 0.18, SigLIP words to photo 0.21, EG2 words to text 0.21 and **kind 0.40** (the item's EG2 text vector against the average text vector of the photo's 5 nearest photos' items, computed inside the same SQL statement with `l2_normalize(avg(...))`, pgvector 0.8.2 checked on the real database). Spreads: text 0.0158, words to photo 0.0136, kind **0.05 fixed**, photo to photo its own. Companies with photo + words: the words alone search them (token ignored); a token without `q` is still a 400. Search log writes `[photo] <words>`. Files: `src/search/photo-words.ts` (+spec), `fusion.ts` (`fuseLists`), `photo-search.ts` (`nearPhoto`), `smart-search.service.ts` (`rankPhotoWords`, `nearestPhotos`), `search-profile.ts` (`photoWords`), `feed.service.ts`, `list-feed.dto.ts`. All numbers are settings `SMART_SEARCH_PHOTO_WORDS_*` (`_POOL`, `_DROP`, `_KIND_NEIGHBOURS`, `_WEIGHT_IMAGE|WORDS_PHOTO|TEXT|KIND`, `_SPREAD_IMAGE|KIND`); `..._WEIGHT_KIND=0` switches the kind list off.
**Frontend:** the search box stays next to the photo chip (placeholder "Add words..."; on Companies "Search companies..."), `lib/feed.ts` `feedQuery` sends `q` with `photo_token`, the lists pass `q: search` with the token, the Companies list shows its notice only for a photo WITHOUT words.
**Measured on the 500-product pool** (250 ABO home goods + 250 Fashion200k, real So400m photos and EG2 texts; 520 cases from 104 labelled searches, nDCG@10, "switch" cases = a photo of the right kind with ANOTHER attribute): photo search alone 15.9, words alone 32.1, three-list cascade 46.3, with the kind list 54.9 and 73% of the top 10 of the photo's kind (66% without it; the photo alone 74%). Chosen on half of the searches the best settings scored the same on the other half. A wider drop (0.26-0.30) alone put a t-shirt first for a sneaker photo + "black" on the 14 real products: the fixed kind spread 0.05 fixed that. Per kind: SigLIP words win on colours of home goods, EG2 text on fashion words; a detail only in the photo caption is the weak spot (nDCG 25, 48 when the text holds it).
**Experiments that were NOT adopted** (numbers in the notes): one composed SigLIP vector `photo + a x words` alone (the pasted alpha 0.65 / beta 0.35: 22; with scale correction 27-34; best 41.5 with the modality gap corrected) — only useful to CHOOSE candidates (same quality with half the candidates: a speed gain); nested "SigLIP fused first, then EG2" = same as flat (49.6, kind purity 59%); clipping lists to 0..1 (-4); closeness-weighted or more neighbours (no change).
**Best idea NOT built (needs the owner's go):** put the kind into the words with SigLIP **zero-shot** over a fixed list of ~28 kind names ("a photo of a dress"; 84.8% accurate on the 500 photos), query = words + kind, both models: 57.9 / 84% (+3.1 nDCG [0.6, 5.6], +10.9 points of kind purity). Costs: a kind-name list from the catalogue's categories, cached vectors, one more text embedding per search. The owner said no extra table rows or embeddings, so it was left; the oracle (true kind word) gives 62.
**Test caveat:** the test pool has catalogue-style photos; real phone photos score lower (the photo lines 0.46 / 0.41 / 0.22 were measured on studio-like data) and Serbian words were not tried with the SigLIP text side.
